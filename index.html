A little about us

Our Story

Get to know the people behind The Pack Shack.

How it all started

We are TY Enterprise students. Our business started when we were thinking, "What would we wish we had in first year?" Study packs, so we made some and much more!

We wanted to create useful, thoughtful and affordable packs for students, with a little something for everyone. From study essentials to sweet treats, there's something for you at The Pack Shack.

Where can I collect my order?

We arrange meetups at school. You can choose the school entrance, sports hall or suggest another suitable place when ordering. We don't offer home deliveries.
